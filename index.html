Quick answers before you reach out.

What is The Backend Reset?
A 2-week intensive cleanup and audit package designed for high-volume Shopify store owners. It covers product data cleanup, inventory discrepancies and support workflows in Gorgias.
Who is Untethered Ops for?
Growth-minded e-commerce founders, Shopify store managers who are drowning in inventory and products, and Gorgias users with overwhelmed support inboxes.
What is included in E-Com Care Essentials?
Dedicated e-commerce backend support that focuses exclusively on Shopify inventory health and Gorgias customer ticket resolution.
What other services do you offer?
Four pillars: E-Com Ops (Shopify maintenance and Gorgias tickets), Data Management (database entry, expense tracking, digital filing), Executive Support (inbox management, scheduling, document prep) and Digital Research (web research, product categorization, asset setup).

Do you work with local and international clients?
Yes. We support e-commerce businesses locally and around the world. Tell us your country or time zone in the form below and we will reply by email to plan how we work together.
How much does it cost?
It depends on your store and the problems we find, so every price is a custom quote. Send us a few details about what feels out of order and we will reply with a quote.
